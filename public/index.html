03 / Our Story

A little
about us.

There are two of us behind Ninety Four. One can spend hours thinking about a small design detail. The other will have made friends with everyone in the room by then.

That balance shapes how we want to host: care in the coffee and everything around it, with a welcome that feels personal.

Our Persian roots inform the flavors we’re exploring and the way we bring people together. Basketball gives us a shared reference, right down to our name: 94 feet, the length of a court.

We’re starting with the cart. It’s a way to bring our attention to coffee, design, and hospitality into the same place.
