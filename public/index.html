A place in motion

Ninety Four

Ninety Four started with two people who couldn't be more different.

One of us is reserved and detail-obsessed, always pulling from fashion, basketball, food, music, design, and the things around us to think about what Ninety Four could become. The other is naturally outgoing, finding energy in people, relationships, family, and bringing others together. One tends to think about the world we're creating; the other makes sure we're thinking about the people who will fill it.

Those differences have shaped what Ninety Four is becoming.

It's a place for all of our interests to exist together without forcing them into one category. Coffee and food. Fashion and objects. Basketball, music, art, and whatever we find ourselves interested in next. Some of it comes from where we come from. Some from what we love. And some will come from the people we meet along the way.

We want to make things worth caring about, but just as importantly, we want to build something people feel part of.

That's Ninety Four.
