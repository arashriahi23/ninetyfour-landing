Ninety Four / Los Angeles

A place
in motion

Ninety Four was built by two people who see the world differently. We share Persian roots, but our experiences, interests, and instincts don’t always overlap. That’s the point.

We created Ninety Four because we believe the most interesting things happen when different worlds meet. Coffee and food. Fashion and objects. Basketball, art and music. Heritage can shape something without defining its limits.

Ninety Four is our attempt to build that room.

Some of it comes from where we come from. Some from what we love. And some from the people we meet along the way.

We don’t believe you have to fit into one world to know who you are. Your True North isn’t a box you fit into. It’s the direction you find by following what means something to you.

What pulls you toward yours?
